The same four decisions on every project.

They are why the sites stay fast and why a change request takes an hour instead of a day.

Design it before building it

Layout goes into Figma first, at real breakpoints. What goes live is what was designed, not whatever the page builder happened to allow on the day.

Write the CSS by hand, scoped

Sections get their own classes and their own rules. The markup stays predictable and nothing turns into a specificity war with builder styles.

Drop to real code where the builder stops

A page builder handles about 90 percent of a homepage well. For the last 10 percent I write the plugin or the front end myself, rather than changing the design to fit the tool.

Build components once, reuse them

Booking funnel, comparison table, carousels, FAQ accordion and lead forms are one component system re-skinned across six verticals. New sites take days instead of weeks.
